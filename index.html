About LeadsUp LLC

LeadsUp LLC is an outsourced appointment-setting (calling) company. Through LeadsUp for Contractors, a roofing or solar contractor hires a dedicated LeadsUp caller who works that contractor's service area by phone for the contractor's own company, talks with homeowners who are thinking about a new roof or solar panels, and books inspection appointments directly onto that contractor's calendar. Each caller works for one contractor; we don't sell leads or homeowner contact lists. Before a contractor decides whether to hire us, we prepare a free, area-level breakdown of their service area so the conversation starts with real numbers.

We are owner-operated by founder Adnan Ahmed, registered in Wyoming, and we work with contractors across the United States. Our program for real estate agents is LeadsUp for Agents at leadsupagents.com. Every text we send goes to a contractor who asked for it on this page; we do not text on behalf of anyone else, and we never sell or share your contact details. Questions?
