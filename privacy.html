Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · LeadsUp LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how LeadsUp LLC ("LeadsUp," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with us through this website, by phone, by SMS, or by email. We handle your information responsibly and transparently as we provide our own appointment-setting and market-research services to roofing and solar contractors.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide: name, work email, company name, type of work (roofing or solar), service area, and mobile number (optional).
  • Communications with us (form submissions, calls, texts, emails).
  • Basic website usage data.

2. How we use it

  • Prepare and deliver your requested service-area breakdown.
  • Provide and schedule our appointment-setting service.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • If you opt in, send SMS about your breakdown, appointments, service updates, and offers for our own services.

3. SMS / text messaging program

If you provide a mobile number and check the SMS consent box on our form, you consent to receive recurring text messages from LeadsUp LLC regarding our services, offers, your service-area breakdown, appointment scheduling, and account or service updates. Providing your number and consenting to texts are both optional and are not a condition of any purchase or service.

When you opt in to texts, we keep a record of your consent: the exact consent wording shown, the date and time, your IP address and your browser's user agent.

Message types: marketing and promotional offers, appointment and account updates, and customer support. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time; you will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Help: Reply HELP, or contact us at +1 (646) 640-0558.

Your mobile information is never sold or shared. No mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in and consent data will be sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. This SMS opt-in information is excluded from every other category of data sharing described in this Privacy Policy.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share limited information only with:

  • Service providers who process it strictly on our behalf (e.g., hosting, email, our messaging platform) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal / safety requirements, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

SMS opt-in and consent data are excluded from all sharing above and are never sold, rented, or shared.

5. Your choices

  • Opt out of SMS, email, or calls at any time (reply STOP, use an unsubscribe link, or contact us).
  • Request access to or deletion of your information.

6. Data security & retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
